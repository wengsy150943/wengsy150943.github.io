## Introduction

Weng Siyang is a PhD candidate in Data Science and Engineering at East China Normal University (ECNU), expected to graduate in 2027. He received a BS degree in Data Science and Big Data Technology from ECNU in 2022. Since Oct 2024, he has been a storage engine R&D intern at Tencent TDSQL, working on transaction correctness verification and storage engine optimization. He has also helped find and fix 80+ database bugs. His research interests include database testing and correctness verification, transaction processing, distributed storage, and storage engine design.
